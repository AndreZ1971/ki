# 🌍 Language Switcher User Guide

**Quick Reference for End Users**

---

## 🇩🇪 🇬🇧 How to Change Your Language

### Finding the Language Switcher

The language flags are located in the **top-right corner** of your ARI dashboard:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Dashboard    Products    Analytics ... 🇩🇪🇬🇧 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                        ↑
                              Here are the flags!
```

### Changing Language (3 Easy Steps)

1. **Look for the flags** 🇩🇪 🇬🇧 in the top-right corner
2. **Click the flag** of the language you want
3. **Done!** Your app is now in that language

That's it! Your choice is automatically saved. 😊

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### The Flags Aren't Visible

**Try this:**
1. Refresh the page (press F5 or Cmd+R)
2. Look in the top-right corner of the page
3. If still not visible, try a different browser

### Language Doesn't Change When I Click

**Try this:**
1. Refresh the page
2. Look for the flags again
3. Click slowly and deliberately
4. Make sure you're clicking the right flag

### My Language Setting Didn't Save

**Try this:**
1. Check if your browser allows cookies/localStorage
   - Chrome: Settings > Privacy > Cookies
   - Firefox: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies
   - Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies

2. Try clearing your browser cache:
   - Chrome: Ctrl+Shift+Delete
   - Firefox: Ctrl+Shift+Delete
   - Safari: Safari > Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data

3. Try again from the beginning

### Text Is Overlapping or Cut Off

**Try this:**
1. Refresh the page
2. Try a different browser
3. Check your browser zoom level (should be 100%)
