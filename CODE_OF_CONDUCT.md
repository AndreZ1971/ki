# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in this project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions.
- Focus on technical substance.
- Accept feedback professionally.
- Help keep discussions solution-oriented.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or repeated hostile behavior.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Deliberate disruption of project workflows.
- Spam, trolling, or bad-faith escalation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- Repository discussions (issues, PRs, comments, reviews)
- Project-linked community channels
- Any official project communication context

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take actions including:

- Warning
- Comment/thread moderation
- Temporary or permanent participation restrictions
- Removal of offending content

Enforcement decisions are made to protect community health and project continuity.

## Reporting

Report behavior concerns via the contact channels in SUPPORT.md.
Include links, timestamps, and relevant context when possible.

## Good-Faith Participation

Mistakes happen. We encourage correction, learning, and respectful resolution when possible.
